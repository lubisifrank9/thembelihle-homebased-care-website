Welcome

Welcome to Thembelihle Homebased Care

Thembelihle Homebased Care is a community centre in Mangweni, Kwalugedlane that gives hope to children who have lost one or both parents, children living alone, and children who have suffered abuse. We work so that every child can enjoy the same rights as any other child: safety, food, education and care.

Our caregivers cook for the children after school, help with homework, and offer daily advice and support. Children are also given food to take home for supper, and food parcels, including during school holidays and the festive season.

We work hand in hand with local schools and teachers, social workers, and child and youth care workers, so that every child is seen, protected and supported. Serious cases are reported to management and referred to social workers.

Who we support

  • Orphans and children who lost both parents
  • Vulnerable children living without parents
  • Children who have suffered abuse
  • People living with disabilities
  • Families in need of food parcels

Our objectives

What we work to achieve

🍲

Feed every child

A cooked meal after school, food to take home and parcels in the holidays.

🛡

Keep children safe

A safe, caring place, with cases of abuse reported and referred to social workers.

📚

Support education

Homework help, daily guidance and advice with life's problems.

👕

Restore dignity

Clothing, school items and practical help so children can thrive like any other child.

Why it matters

For many children, this may be their only meal of the day

Without a parent at home, a child can face hunger, danger, abuse and emotional distress. Our centre gives children a daily routine of food, care and someone who listens.

  • No child goes hungry
  • No child feels alone
  • Every child has hope

A message to donors and partners

We run on the generosity of individuals, businesses, retailers, companies and government. Our caregivers also depend on donations to be paid. Without support, the centre could close and the children would be left in danger.

Every donation, big or small, keeps our kitchen open.
