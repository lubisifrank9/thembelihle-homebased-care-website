Our Team & Partners

We work hand in hand with local schools and teachers, social workers, and child and youth care workers, so every child is seen, protected, and supported.

Our partners

Working with the social development office

Thembelihle Homebased Care currently receives funding from the social development office to keep us running, and we appreciate working with them as our initial funder.

With their support throughout the year, we are able to meet the needs the centre needs the most.

Who we work with

  • Local schools and teachers
  • Social workers
  • Child and youth care workers
  • Clinics
  • Social development office (initial funder)

Why your donation matters

We run on generosity

We run entirely on the generosity of individuals, businesses, retailers, companies, and government. Our caregivers also depend on donations to be paid. Without support, children face hunger, danger, abuse, and emotional distress, and the centre could close.
